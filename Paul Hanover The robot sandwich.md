# How to make a sandwich

## Assumptions Section
The robot is at a ready state with hands empty. The two slices of bread are on the counter along with all the contents to make the sandwich. Mayanaise jar, mustard, cheese, Romaine lettuce and tomatoe. The meat we will be using is roast beef and fried bacon. You will have the utensils needed to also slice or spread the mayonaise and mustard on the sandwich.

## Step by step instructions for putting putting the sandwich together
1. unscrew the mayonnaise jar and lay the lid on the counter face up
2. grab the butter knife provided and stick it in the jar and scoop a generous amount out onto one of the slices of bread and spread it evenly
3. repeat step 2 for the second slice of bread
4. rinse off and slice up some romaine lettuce with the other knife width wise creating little ribbons of lettuce
5. rinse off the tomato and slice up a couple slices
6. grab a slice of pre-sliced cheese from our supplies and place it on one of the slices of bread
7. put the sliced tomato on the cheese you just laid on the slice of bread
8. next grab some of the sliced up lettuce and place on the tomato
9. grab three slices of thinly sliced roast beef and evenly lay it on the slice of bread that doesn't have anything on it yet
10. grab a couple of slices of bacon and lay it on top of the roast beef you just laid on the slice of bread
11. put both slices of bread together by grabbing the slice with meet and bacon and putting it on top of the the other prepared slice of bread
12. oncew both slices are put together with all the contents in it you can now grab the sharpest knife you have and cut the sandwich in half by slicing it diagonally creating two triangles

"Done When" you have to diagonally equivalent halves with all the contents instructed in steps 1 thru 12
